Know your piece.

Explore the material, fit and care information for your Aayam jewellery.

Gold colour

Aayam's range uses yellow, rose and white gold. Rose gold gets its warmer tone from a higher copper content in the alloy; white gold is typically rhodium-plated for its bright finish, which can wear and be reapplied over time. Not every piece is offered in every colour — each product page lists only the colours actually available for that design.

To confirm before release: the exact alloy composition and any plating used per product, and Aayam's hallmarking/purity certification details.

Karat

Karat describes how much of a piece is pure gold: 24K is pure gold, 22K is 22 parts gold to 2 parts other metals, and so on down to 9K. A higher karat is softer and more yellow in tone; a lower karat is harder and better suited to pieces worn daily. Aayam offers 9K, 14K, 18K and 22K depending on the piece — each product page shows only the karats it's actually offered in.

Finding your fit

Rings: if you know a recent ring size, use it. Otherwise, wrap a strip of paper or thread snugly around the base of the finger, mark where it overlaps, and measure the length against a ruler — that measurement in millimetres roughly maps to a standard ring-size chart. Sizing runs slightly differently across styles, so if you're unsure, choose "I need sizing help" on the product page instead of guessing.

Bracelets: measure around the wrist where you'd normally wear a bracelet, then add roughly 1–1.5cm for comfortable movement. Aayam's bracelet lengths are offered as S (6.5"), M (7") and L (7.5").

Chains and pendants: shorter lengths (18") sit at the collarbone; longer lengths (22–24") sit lower on the chest. If you already own a chain you like the length of, measuring it end to end is the most reliable comparison.

To confirm before release: Aayam's own sizing system if it differs from the general guidance above, and whether in-person resizing is offered.

Care

As a general rule for fine gold jewellery: remove pieces before swimming, exercising or manual work; avoid contact with perfume, lotion and chlorinated water, which can dull the finish over time; and store pieces separately, ideally in a soft pouch, to prevent surface scratching. Specific stones (onyx, malachite, spinel, aventurine) need gentler handling — each product page's Fit & Care section notes anything specific to that piece.

To confirm before release: any manufacturer-specific cleaning or servicing instructions from Payal Jewellers.
