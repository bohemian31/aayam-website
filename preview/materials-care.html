Materials & Care

Know what you are choosing.

A little clarity about material, fit and care makes it easier to find a piece that feels right.

This is general guidance for a design preview. The photographed pieces have not been verified as Aayam products. Their listed materials, sizes, prices and care details are samples, not product facts.

01 / Material

Colour and karat are different things.

Yellow, rose and white describe the appearance of gold jewellery. Karat describes its gold content. Two pieces may share a colour while differing in composition, and a photograph alone cannot establish either.

Finishes and any surface treatments also vary by piece. Before choosing, ask for the exact metal, karat, finish and any other material used in the design.

The preview's colour and karat selectors demonstrate a possible catalogue layout. They do not confirm which options exist for a photographed piece.

02 / Gold content

What karat tells you.

Gold karat expresses the share of gold in an alloy out of 24 parts. For example, 18K indicates 18 parts gold out of 24; the remainder is other metals. It is one useful fact, but it does not describe the full design, its finish or how it should be cared for.

The draft catalogue includes sample 9K, 14K, 18K and 22K options so the preview can demonstrate product selection. Actual options and purity information must be confirmed for each piece.

03 / Fit

Find your measure.

Rings

A ring that already fits the intended finger is the best starting point. Share its size, or ask for help measuring if you are unsure. Band width and profile can affect how a ring feels.

Bracelets

Measure where you prefer a bracelet to sit, then say whether you like a close or relaxed fit. An open cuff and a linked bracelet may need different measurements.

Chains & pendants

Measure a chain you already like from end to end and compare its length and fall. Ask whether the chain is included with a pendant before deciding.

Sizes displayed on product pages are sample data. The final size guide will follow verified measurements for each design.

04 / Care

Make room for the details.

As a general habit, store pieces separately to reduce rubbing and surface marks. Set jewellery aside for activities where it could be struck or caught, and keep it away from products that may leave residue.

Cleaning depends on the actual metal, finish and any stones or inlays. Ask for instructions specific to the piece before using cleaners, polishing cloths or ultrasonic equipment.
