Fine Jewellery. Made for you.

Some things become part of you.

Aayam means “dimension” in Hindi. Our name speaks to the many dimensions of a man: his taste, his experiences, the things he chooses to carry. Fine jewellery gives them a form he can wear.

The making of Aayam

What you wear carries the work of many hands.

Shape, proportion, finish: each choice brings a piece closer to the person who will wear it. We make with Payal Jewellers & Casting in Rajkot, Gujarat.

01 / Our foundations

A new point of view.
A practiced hand.

Aayam brings a contemporary eye to men’s jewellery, with an established making tradition behind it. In Rajkot, Payal Jewellers & Casting brings more than 18 years of experience to our pieces. New ideas find their form in hands that know the work.

02 / The form

Presence is a matter of proportion.

The width of a band. The fall of a chain. The space a bracelet leaves at the wrist. These choices shape how jewellery sits within a look, leaving room for a quiet detail, a stronger gesture, and everything between.

03 / The detail

The closer you look, the more it says.

A polished surface catches the room. A softer finish holds the light differently. An edge changes the way a piece feels against the skin. Its character emerges slowly, through touch and the familiarity of wearing it.

04 / In your keeping

Confidence comes with clarity.

Authenticity is a starting point for us. We confirm a piece’s material and purity, share its applicable hallmark and certification information, and inspect it before delivery. We also guide you on fit, resizing and care, with a real team to turn to after it becomes yours.

Our making partner

Experience, carried forward.

Payal Jewellers & Casting brings complete manufacturing capability to Aayam from Rajkot, Gujarat. Its 18+ years in jewellery making and long-standing relationships with retailers are part of the foundation we build on.
