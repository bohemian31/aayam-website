Privacy & analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors discover and use our website. In the European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your choice before analytics starts. We also ask when we cannot determine your location. In other locations, analytics starts without a banner unless you have previously turned it off in this browser.

What we measure

When enabled, we measure page visits, general location, device type, traffic source, product and category views, searches and filters, gallery choices, and clicks to enquire by WhatsApp or email. We do not use these clicks as proof that a message was sent. We do not intentionally send your name, email address, phone number, or message text to analytics. Search terms are not sent to analytics.

Your choice

You can turn analytics off or change a previous choice at any time using the button below. Your choice is saved in this browser. Turning analytics off stops future tracking on this browser; browser settings can be used to clear cookies already set. Your location is determined from your network address for this regional setting.

When you contact us

WhatsApp and email open their own services. Information you send in a conversation is handled separately from website analytics. Contact us at thehouseofaayam@gmail.com with privacy questions.
